# 📘 Lesson 6 – *A család*

**The family**

## 🎯 Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you'll know how to:

* talk about **family members**,
* use **possessive forms** with family vocabulary,
* use **van/nincs** (have/don't have) with family,
* describe people with **adjectives**,
* ask questions about family: **Hány...?** (How many...?),
* use **numbers** to talk about family size and ages.

## 2️⃣ Possessive forms with family

When talking about YOUR family, add the possessive suffix:

| Base word | My... |
| ---------- | ---------- |
| anya | anyá**m** (my mother) |
| apa | apá**m** (my father) |
| fiú | fiá**m** (my son) |
| lány | lányá**m** (my daughter) |
| testvér | testvér**em** (my sibling) |
| báty | bátyá**m** (my older brother) |
| öcs | öcsé**m** (my younger brother) |
| nővér | nővér**em** (my older sister) |
| húg | húgá**m** (my younger sister) |

### Examples:

* **Az anyám tanár.**
  My mother is a teacher.

* **A bátyám Budapesten lakik.**
  My older brother lives in Budapest.

* **A húgom tíz éves.**
  My younger sister is 10 years old.

---

## 3️⃣ Using **van** and **nincs** with family

### **Van** = to have (literally "there is")

* **Van egy bátyám.**
  I have an older brother.

* **Van két testvérem.**
  I have two siblings.

* **Vannak gyerekeim.**
  I have children.

---

### **Nincs** = don't have (literally "there isn't")

* **Nincs testvérem.**
  I don't have siblings.

* **Nincsenek gyerekeim.**
  I don't have children.

---

### Structure comparison

| Positive | Negative |
| -------- | -------- |
| Van egy bátyám | Nincs bátyám |
| Van húgom | Nincs húgom |
| Vannak testvéreim | Nincsenek testvéreim |

## 4️⃣ Asking about family

### **Hány...?** – "How many...?"

* **Hány testvéred van?**
  How many siblings do you have?

* **Hány gyereked van?**
  How many children do you have?

* **Hány éves az apád?**
  How old is your father?

---

### Other family questions

* **Van testvéred?**
  Do you have siblings?

* **Nagycsalád a tiéd?**
  Is yours a big family?

* **Hol laknak a szüleid?**
  Where do your parents live?

* **Mit csinál az anyád?**
  What does your mother do?

### Examples in sentences

* **Az apám magas és kedves.**
  My father is tall and kind.

* **A nagymamám idős, de nagyon vicces.**
  My grandmother is elderly but very funny.

  My older brother is smart and hardworking.

* **A húgom még fiatal.**
  My younger sister is still young.

## 7️⃣ Numbers with family

Review: Using numbers to talk about family size and ages

* **Két testvérem van.**
  I have two siblings.

* **Három gyerekem van.**
  I have three children.

* **Az anyám ötven éves.**
  My mother is 50 years old.

* **Négyen vagyunk a családban.**
  There are four of us in the family.

👉 **-en/-an/-ön** suffix = counting people (hárman = three people, négyen = four people)

## 9️⃣ Model dialogues

### Dialogue 1: Talking about siblings

**A:** Van testvéred?
**B:** Igen, van egy bátyám és egy húgom.
**A:** Hány évesek?
**B:** A bátyám huszonöt éves, a húgom pedig tizenhat.
**A:** Mit csinálnak?
**B:** A bátyám dolgozik, a húgom még iskolába jár.

*A: Do you have siblings?*
*B: Yes, I have an older brother and a younger sister.*
*A: How old are they?*
*B: My older brother is 25, and my younger sister is 16.*
*A: What do they do?*
*B: My brother works, my sister still goes to school.*

---

### Dialogue 2: Describing family

**A:** Milyen a családod?
**B:** Kicsi a családom. Négyen vagyunk: az anyám, az apám, a húgom és én.
**A:** Mit csinálnak a szüleid?
**B:** Az anyám tanár, az apám mérnök.
**A:** És a húgod?
**B:** Ő még kicsi, tíz éves.

*A: What's your family like?*
*B: My family is small. There are four of us: my mother, my father, my younger sister and me.*
*A: What do your parents do?*
*B: My mother is a teacher, my father is an engineer.*
*A: And your younger sister?*
*B: She's still little, ten years old.*

## 🔟 Key grammatical points

### Possessive with family members

Remember vowel harmony!

**Back vowels (a, o, u):**
- anya → anyá**m**
- apa → apá**m**
- báty → bátyá**m**

**Front vowels (e, i, ö, ü):**
- testvér → testvér**em**
- nővér → nővér**em**
- öcs → öcsé**m**

---

### Plural possessive

When talking about "my parents," "my children," etc.:

| Singular | Plural possessive |
| -------- | ----------------- |
| apám | szülei**m** (my parents) |
| gyerekem | gyerekei**m** (my children) |
| testvérem | testvérei**m** (my siblings) |

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Key points to remember

* Hungarian distinguishes between **older** and **younger** siblings
* Use **van** (have) and **nincs** (don't have) to talk about family
* **Hány...?** asks "how many?"
* Add possessive suffixes to family words: anyá**m**, apá**m**, etc.
* Use **-en/-an/-ön** to count people: hárman, négyen
* Vowel harmony applies to all possessive suffixes
