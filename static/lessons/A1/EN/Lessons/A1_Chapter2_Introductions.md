# Lesson 2 — Meetings  
## Introducing yourself and exchanging basic information

In this lesson, you'll learn to **introduce yourself in Hungarian**, **say hello and goodbye**, use the **verb "to be" in the present tense**, and ask **simple questions**: language spoken, place of residence, age, profession.

---

## 1. Saying hello and goodbye

### Greetings

| English | Hungarian | Pronunciation |
|--------|----------|---------------|
| Hello (formal) | Jó napot! | *yo nopot* |
| Hi | Szia! | *see-ah* |
| Hi (plural / formal) | Sziasztok! | *see-ahs-tok* |
| Good evening | Jó estét! | *yo esh-tayt* |
| Good night | Jó éjszakát! | *yo ay-sah-kaht* |

💡 **Szia** is used both for *hello* and *goodbye*.

---

### Saying goodbye

| English | Hungarian |
|--------|----------|
| Goodbye (formal) | Viszontlátásra |
| Bye / See you | Szia |
| See you soon | Hamarosan találkozunk |

---

## 2. The verb "to be" in present tense

In Hungarian, the verb **lenni** (*to be*) is **often omitted** in the 3rd person,  
but **present in the 1st and 2nd person**.

| English | Hungarian |
|--------|----------|
| I am | **én vagyok** |
| You are | **te vagy** |
| He / she is | (ő) van *(often omitted)* |
| We are | mi vagyunk |
| You are (plural) | ti vagytok |
| They are | (ők) vannak |

### Examples

- **Én vagyok Anna.**  
  *I am Anna.*

- **Te diák vagy.**  
  *You are a student.*

- **Budapesten élek.**  
  *I live in Budapest.*  

---

## 3. Introducing yourself simply

### Stating your name

- **Én Péter vagyok.**  
  I am Péter.

- **A nevem Marie.**  
  My name is Marie.

---

### Saying where you live

- **Budapesten élek.**  
  I live in Budapest.

- **Franciaországban élek.**  
  I live in France.

📌 The suffix **-ban / -ben** means *in*.

---

## 4. Talking about languages

### Asking the question

**Milyen nyelven beszélsz?**  
*What language do you speak?*

### Possible answers

- **Franciául beszélek.**  
  I speak French.

- **Magyarul tanulok.**  
  I'm learning Hungarian.

- **Angolul is beszélek.**  
  I also speak English.

💡 The suffix **-ul / -ül** indicates the language.

---

## 5. Stating your age

### Asking the question

**Hány éves vagy?**  
*How old are you?*

### Answering

- **Húsz éves vagyok.**  
  I am 20 years old.

📌 Literally: *I am 20-years-old.*

---

## 6. Stating your profession or status

### Student / Teacher

- **Diák vagyok.**  
  I am a student.

- **Tanár vagyok.**  
  I am a teacher.

- **Egyetemista vagyok.**  
  I am a university student.

---

## 7. Model dialogue

**A:** Szia!  
**B:** Szia!  
**A:** Én Pierre vagyok. Te hogy hívnak?  
**B:** Anna vagyok.  
**A:** Budapesten élsz?  
**B:** Igen, Budapesten élek. És te?  
**A:** Franciaországban élek, de magyarul tanulok.

---

## 8. Key points

- **Szia** = hello *and* goodbye
- The verb **to be** is often invisible in Hungarian
- **-ban / -ben** = in
- **-ul / -ül** = spoken language
- Hungarian is **logical and regular**
