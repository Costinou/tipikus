# 📘 Lesson 5 – *A városban*

**In the city**

## 🎯 Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you'll know how to:

* use **menni** (to go) in present tense,
* ask questions with **hol** (where), **mikor** (when), **hova** (where to),
* understand **question structure** in Hungarian,
* use essential **city vocabulary**,
* understand the difference between **-ban/-ben** (in) and **-ba/-be** (to/into).

---

## 1️⃣ The verb **menni** – "to go"

Present tense conjugation:

| Person | menni | English |
| --------- | --------- | --------- |
| I | **megyek** | I go |
| you | **mész** | you go |
| he / she | **megy** | he/she goes |
| we | **megyünk** | we go |
| you (plural) | **mentek** | you go |
| they | **mennek** | they go |

### Examples:

* **A boltba megyek.**
  I'm going to the store.

* **Péter a moziba megy.**
  Péter is going to the cinema.

* **Holnap a parkba megyünk.**
  Tomorrow we're going to the park.

👉 Notice: The verb **menni** is irregular, so you need to memorize these forms.

## 4️⃣ Question structure in Hungarian

In Hungarian, questions are formed in two main ways:

### Type 1: With question words

The question word usually comes **first**, followed by the verb.

**Structure:** Question word + verb + subject + rest

Examples:

* **Hol van a posta?**
  Where is the post office?

* **Mikor mész a moziba?**
  When are you going to the cinema?

* **Hova mennek?**
  Where are they going?

* **Mi van a táskában?**
  What's in the bag?

---

### Type 2: Yes/No questions

For yes/no questions, Hungarian uses the **-e** suffix or changes word order by placing the verb first.

Examples:

* **Mész a boltba?**
  Are you going to the store?

* **Péter tanár?**
  Is Péter a teacher?

* **Van közel bank?**
  Is there a bank nearby?

👉 You can answer with **Igen** (yes) or **Nem** (no).

## 5️⃣ **Hol** vs. **Hova** – Location vs. Direction

This is a crucial distinction in Hungarian!

### **Hol?** – Where? (static location)

Use with **-ban / -ben** (in)

* **Hol vagy?**
  Where are you?

* **A könyvtárban vagyok.**
  I'm in the library.

* **Hol van a bank?**
  Where is the bank?

---

### **Hova?** – Where to? (direction/movement)

Use with **-ba / -be** (to/into)

* **Hova mész?**
  Where are you going?

* **A könyvtárba megyek.**
  I'm going to the library.

* **Hova megy Péter?**
  Where is Péter going?

---

### Comparison table

| Question | Answer with location | Answer with direction |
| -------- | -------------------- | --------------------- |
| **Hol?** | a bolt**ban** | — |
| **Hova?** | — | a bolt**ba** |
| **Hol?** | a parkban | — |
| **Hova?** | — | a parkba |
| **Hol?** | az iskol**ában** | — |
| **Hova?** | — | az iskol**ába** |

👉 Remember: **-ban/-ben** = IN (location), **-ba/-be** = TO/INTO (direction)

## 6️⃣ Using **Mikor?** – "When?"

**Mikor?** asks about time.

### Examples:

* **Mikor mész a moziba?**
  When are you going to the cinema?

* **Ma este megyek.**
  I'm going this evening.

* **Mikor van a koncert?**
  When is the concert?

* **Holnap van.**
  It's tomorrow.

## 9️⃣ Model dialogues

### Dialogue 1: Asking for directions

**A:** Elnézést, hol van a posta?
**B:** A posta a téren van.
**A:** Messze van?
**B:** Nem, közel van. Öt perc.
**A:** Köszönöm!

*A: Excuse me, where is the post office?*
*B: The post office is in the square.*
*A: Is it far?*
*B: No, it's close. Five minutes.*
*A: Thank you!*

---

### Dialogue 2: Making plans

**A:** Hova mész ma este?
**B:** A moziba megyek. És te?
**A:** Én a barátnőmmel étterembe megyek.
**B:** Melyik étterembe?
**A:** Az új olasz étterembe.

*A: Where are you going this evening?*
*B: I'm going to the cinema. And you?*
*A: I'm going to a restaurant with my girlfriend.*
*B: Which restaurant?*
*A: To the new Italian restaurant.*

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Key points to remember

* **menni** is irregular: megyek, mész, megy...
* **Hol?** + **-ban/-ben** = location (WHERE you ARE)
* **Hova?** + **-ba/-be** = direction (WHERE you're GOING)
* Question words usually come first in Hungarian questions
* **Mikor?** asks about time
* Vowel harmony applies to **-ban/-ben** and **-ba/-be**
