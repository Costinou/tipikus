ℹ️ About Tipikus

Tipikus is a Hungarian learning application based on:

  • Flashcards: Memorize vocabulary effectively (10 XP per card)
  • Quizzes: Test your knowledge (20 XP per correct answer)
  • Exercises: Practice with fill-in exercises (25 XP per correct answer)
  • Progression: Unlock levels progressively with XP

💡 Tip: Earn at least 80% of available XP to unlock the next level!
